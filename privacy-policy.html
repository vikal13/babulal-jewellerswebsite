Effective Date: 17 October 2023

Introduction
Welcome to Babulal Jewellers (hereinafter referred to as "we," "us," or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal data when you interact with our website, products, services, or any other online or offline engagement.

1. Information We Collect
1.1 Personal Information: We may collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, email address, and other identifying details when you provide it to us voluntarily. This information is collected for purposes like communication, customer support, and service delivery.
1.2 Non-Personal Information: We may also gather non-personal data, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information for analytical and statistical purposes.
1.3 Cookies and Tracking: We use cookies and other tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

We may use your information for the following purposes:
2.1 Service Delivery: To provide the products, services, and information you request from us.
2.2 Communication: To respond to your inquiries, provide customer support, and keep you updated about our offerings and promotions.
2.3 Analytics: To understand how our website and services are used and improve their functionality.
2.4 Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We may share your personal information with:
3.1 Service Providers: Third-party service providers who help us deliver our products and services, subject to confidentiality agreements.
3.2 Legal Requirements: To comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests from government authorities.

4. Data Security
We implement reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights
You have certain rights concerning your personal data:
5.1 Access: You may request access to the personal information we hold about you.
5.2 Correction: You may update or correct your personal information.
5.3 Deletion: You can request the deletion of your data under certain circumstances.
5.4 Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.

6. Changes to Privacy Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on our website, and the "Effective Date" will reflect the most recent update.
